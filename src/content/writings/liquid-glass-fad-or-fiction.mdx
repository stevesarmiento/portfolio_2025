## The shine wears off fast

Every era of UI has its signature material — brushed metal, flat color, frosted
blur. Liquid glass is the latest, and the question is whether it's a material
or a mood.

## What it's actually for

Refraction and depth aren't decoration; they're hierarchy. When the chrome
bends the content behind it, the interface tells you what's surface and what's
substance.

## The cost of the effect

Legibility, contrast, battery, and the long tail of devices that can't render
it well. A design language that only works on flagship hardware is a demo, not
a system.
